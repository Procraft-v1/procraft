CV Maker

Create an online CV and a professional public profile

A CV is more than a document — it's your professional story. Procraft organizes your CV data into a public profile that sits close to a portfolio and resume structure, ready to share with one link.

What should a CV include?

A CV should contain your personal details, professional focus, work experience, education, skills, certificates and projects. Most importantly, this information must be easy to read and well organized.

Why is an online CV convenient?

  • A CV link can be shared instantly.
  • When you update your data, the public profile updates too.
  • Portfolio projects make your CV more credible.
  • Templates give your CV a professional look.

How CV and portfolio work together

A plain CV lists your experience, while a portfolio shows the results. Procraft combines both in one dashboard and gives you a public profile, so recruiters can verify your story at a glance.

Which template fits best?

Classic suits formal fields, Minimal works for a simple resume-style look, and Modern fits contemporary digital specialists.
